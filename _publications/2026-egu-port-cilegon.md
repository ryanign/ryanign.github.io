excerpt: "This study presents a user-friendly GUI application for tsunami risk analysis at ports, combining existing fragility curves with tsunami inundation maps and port structure shapefiles to generate automated risk reports covering structural damage, production capacity recovery, and physical loss estimation. The application was preliminarily tested at Cilegon Port, West Java, under a worst-case Mw 8.9 Sunda Megathrust rupture scenario, demonstrating its utility for critical maritime infrastructure risk assessment. The tool is designed to be accessible and transferable across port settings, supporting disaster risk reduction planning for one of the world's most economically vital infrastructure types."

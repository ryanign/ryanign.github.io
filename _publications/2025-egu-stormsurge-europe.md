excerpt: "Despite significant storm surge losses from extratropical cyclones in Europe — including Xynthia (2010), Xaver (2013), and Eunice (2022) — commercial cat models provide limited coverage of European storm surge risk, with most focusing primarily on the UK. This study addresses that gap by developing a scenario-based storm surge framework for North-Western Europe using SCHISM 2D, reproducing 10 significant historical events and generating 480 perturbed scenarios across variations in wind speed, storm size, and tide timing. Loss estimates were derived against a representative North-Western Europe insured market portfolio, providing AXA with a quantified view of storm surge risk exposure across France, Germany, and the United Kingdom."

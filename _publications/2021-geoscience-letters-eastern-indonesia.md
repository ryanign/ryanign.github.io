excerpt: 'Eastern Indonesia hosts some of the world's most diverse tsunami sources — tectonic, submarine mass failure, and volcanic — yet remains critically understudied. This study presents the first multi-source deterministic tsunami hazard assessment for the region, modelling credible scenarios from historical and modern data using state-of-the-art simulation tools. Results reveal potential tsunami amplitudes exceeding 11 m along Sulawesi's west coast and successfully reproduce the 25 m runup documented in the 1871 Ruang volcanic tsunami.'

excerpt: "Following significant insured losses from Typhoons Hato (2017) and Mangkhut (2018), this study develops a catastrophe modelling framework for storm surge and wave hazard across the Philippines and Hong Kong regions. Over 10,000 full-physics coupled storm surge and wave simulations were computed using SCHISM, driven by a synthetic tropical cyclone wind event set and ranked by Integrated Kinetic Energy (IKE) index. Surge and wave footprints were converted to insured losses using adjusted Hazus damage functions, and the relationship between wind and surge losses was analysed to derive inter-peril correlation factors for use in cat model workflows."

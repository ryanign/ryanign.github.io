excerpt: "An Mw7.0 earthquake from Majene, Western Sulawesi, generated an unusually high tsunami that decayed rapidly within 25 km from the highest observed location. Through reanalysis of the earthquake parameters, ground motion modelling, and tsunami simulation, this study shows that the earthquake alone cannot reproduce the observed tsunami. A compound source of earthquake and a submarine mass failure provides the most plausible explanation for the observation."

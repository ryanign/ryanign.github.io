excerpt: "This study develops a high-resolution (30 m) compound flood modelling framework to simulate the interaction of coastal, fluvial, and pluvial flooding driven by tropical cyclones across the continental US, demonstrated for 9 historical TC events in the Greater New Orleans area. The offshore component — storm surge and wave modelling — was handled by SCHISM-WWMIII, providing the coastal boundary conditions that drive onshore inundation. Results show that simulating flood drivers separately and summing them underestimates peak flood disturbance by 60% or more, underscoring the importance of coupled modelling approaches that capture nonlinear compound interactions."

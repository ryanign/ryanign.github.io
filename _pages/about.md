I build coastal flood hazard models — storm surge and tsunami — from simulation framework design through to validated, delivery-ready outputs. With a decade of applied experience across Southeast Asia, Europe, and the Pacific, I have delivered stochastic modelling databases for commercial clients, contributed to national-scale probabilistic hazard assessments, and developed open-source frameworks for large-scale inundation simulation.

My work sits at the intersection of physical science and applied risk — translating complex coastal hazard into outputs that are usable for disaster risk reduction, insurance, and infrastructure planning.

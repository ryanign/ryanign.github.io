## Overview
Nearly 42 million residents live in the greated area of Jakarta ([UN DESA, 2025](https://www.un.org/pl/node/236908)). This region faces significant earthquake risk from shallow crustal faults and the Sunda-Java megathrust. While probabilistic earthquake risk assessment for the region has been conducted ([Damanik et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.1080/19475705.2023.2202805)), a comprehensive tsunami risk asssessment has not. This study extends that work by performing deterministic tsunami risk assessments from plausible megathrust earthquake scenarios, exploring the feasibility of tsunami as an insurable peril and laying the groundwork for potential (re)insurance produce development in Indonesia.

## Methods
There are two main components to this study: (1) a probabilistic earthquake risk assessment conducted by [Damanik et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.1080/19475705.2023.2202805), and (2) deterministic tsunami inundation modelling from three megathrust earthquake scenarios. We applied the two-stage framework of [Pranantyo et al., 2026](https://doi.org/10.5194/egusphere-egu26-10971) to generate high-resolution tsunami inundation footprints. The number of affected residential buildings affected was estimated using the [Global Buildings Footprints](https://github.com/microsoft/GlobalMLBuildingFootprints) from Microsoft.
